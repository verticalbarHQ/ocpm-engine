# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must be professional, respectful, and welcoming. Participants
should discuss ideas and evidence, give actionable feedback, acknowledge
mistakes, respect privacy, and make room for people with different experience
and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, repeated personal attacks, and publishing another person's
private information are unacceptable. Technical disagreement is not a license
for abusive behavior.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project
events, and other spaces where a person represents the project or Vertical Bar,
Inc.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Send a private report
through <https://vertical.bar/contact> with the project name, relevant links,
dates, and the behavior observed. Reports will be limited to people who need
them for review. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, limit
participation, temporarily suspend participation, or permanently exclude a
participant. Responses will consider severity, pattern, impact, safety, and
whether behavior was corrected. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must
not decide the report.

## Good-faith use

This policy protects participation and safety. It must not be used to suppress
good-faith technical criticism, security reports, research findings, or
licensing concerns.
